How to Play Delta Roulette

Delta Roulette is an exciting casino dice game played with two standard six-sided dice.

1. Placing Bets

Select a chip denomination ($1, $5, $10, $25, or $100) from the bottom panel and click any spot on the gaming mat:

  • Pyramid Pairs (Hexagon Tiles): Bet on exact dice pair combinations regardless of order.
    • Mixed Pairs (e.g., 1 & 6, 3 & 5): Pays 15:1
    • Doubles (e.g., 1 & 1, 6 & 6): Pays 30:1
  • Sum Spaces (Bottom Bar): Bet on the combined sum of both rolled dice:
    • Sum ≤ 6: Pays 2:1
    • Sum = 7: Pays 5:1
    • Sum ≥ 8: Pays 2:1

2. Turn Structure

Once bets are placed, click ROLL DICE 🎲 or click directly on the Dice Tray in the top right corner.

3. Winning & Settlement

Winning tiles highlight for 1 second. All winning payouts credit automatically to the active bankrolls, after which winning highlights and placed chips clear from the felt for the next round.
